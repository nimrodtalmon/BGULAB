# Digital Cooperation Lab

We study how humans and AI agents, in varying combinations, reach good
collective decisions together. We draw on computational social choice, AI,
game theory, and combinatorial optimization, with an emphasis on the
blockchain/DAO ecosystem.

Our method: mathematical models stress-tested in simulation,
towards a principled environment for tuning governance parameters (partially
funded by IOG / Cardano).

The work is organized by who cooperates with whom:

## Humans with AI

- Grassroots federation formation

### AI-Agora

Each person has a personal AI agent that finds others, negotiates, and
forms groups on their behalf. People keep the last word: a hard veto over
what their agent agrees to.

### Collective control of AI agents (lab only)

How a group jointly steers an AI agent it shares: which decisions go to the
group, through which channels (configuration, shared memory, runtime
approvals), and how the members' inputs are aggregated. A joint FWF–ISF
proposal with Martin Lackner (USTP) is in preparation; this site is a small
test bed.

## Key terms

For newcomers: the words used on this site, each with one place to start.

### Computational social choice

How groups decide (voting, allocating, choosing committees), studied with
the tools of computer science: algorithms, complexity, simulation. Start
with the [Handbook of Computational Social
Choice](https://www.cambridge.org/9781107060432) (Cambridge, 2016).

### Digital democracy

Democratic decision-making designed for online communities from the ground
up, rather than paper-ballot procedures moved online. A position paper:
[Foundations of e-Democracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02873) (Shapiro,
Communications of the ACM, 2018).

### Liquid democracy

Each voter votes directly or delegates their vote, possibly per topic, to
someone they trust; delegates can delegate further. A survey: [An Overview
of Ten Years of Liquid Democracy
Research](https://doi.org/10.1145/3396956.3396963) (Paulin, 2020). The
best-known system is [LiquidFeedback](https://liquidfeedback.com/en/); its
design is explained in [The Principles of
LiquidFeedback](https://liquidfeedback.com/pub/The_Principles_of_LiquidFeedback_1st_edition_online_version.pdf)
(Behrens et al., 2014).

### Sortition (lot)

Choosing decision-makers at random, as in citizens' assemblies. The
algorithmic question is how to draw a panel that is representative and
still gives every volunteer a fair chance: [Fair algorithms for selecting
citizens' assemblies](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03788-6)
(Flanigan et al., Nature, 2021).

### DAO

A decentralized autonomous organization: an organization owned and
governed collectively by its members, on a blockchain. An introduction:
[ethereum.org on DAOs](https://ethereum.org/en/dao/).
